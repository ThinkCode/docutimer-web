DocuTimer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Publisher: BYTEXIO LLC · Contact: support@bytexio.com

Summary

DocuTimer does not collect your data. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your documents, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Your information stays on your device, and, only if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud database.

What the app stores

  • Document records you enter or scan (names, issuing authority, dates, document numbers, notes, fees, reminders) and optional household member names. These are stored on your device.
  • Scans and photos you attach. They are encrypted on your device (AES-GCM) with a key kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Preferences such as appearance, currency and reminder time, stored on your device.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud sync, DocuTimer copies your records to your private CloudKit database in your own iCloud account so your devices stay in sync. Sensitive fields are stored as CloudKit encrypted fields. BYTEXIO LLC cannot read, access or recover this data. Turn sync off in Settings ▸ iCloud Sync; manage iCloud storage in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

  • Camera — to scan documents. Images are processed on your device (Apple Vision) and are not sent to us.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — to lock the app. Biometric data is handled by iOS; the app never receives it.
  • Notifications — local reminders scheduled on your device.
  • Calendar — only if you tap “Add to Calendar” on a document.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — only when you use voice in Ask; recognition runs on your device.

Apple Intelligence (Ask)

On supported devices, the Ask feature uses Apple’s on-device model. Questions are processed on your device and are not sent to BYTEXIO LLC.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Subscriptions are managed in Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions.

Sharing and backups

If you export a backup or share a document, the file goes where you choose. Backups are encrypted with the password you set; we cannot recover that password.

Tracking and data sale

We do not track you, do not sell data and do not share data with third parties.

Children

DocuTimer is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove local data. To remove iCloud copies, turn off sync and manage DocuTimer data in iOS Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. Questions: support@bytexio.com.
