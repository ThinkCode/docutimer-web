DocuTimer Support

Email support@bytexio.com and include your iPhone/iPad model, iOS version and the DocuTimer version (Settings ▸ bottom of the screen). We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings ▸ Restore Purchases (or “Restore” on the Pro screen). Use the same Apple Account that bought it.

How do I cancel the annual plan?

iOS Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions ▸ DocuTimer Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I changed phones. Where are my documents?

Turn on Settings ▸ iCloud Sync on the old phone first, or export an encrypted backup (Settings ▸ Backup) and import it on the new phone. Scan images are encrypted with a key that stays on the original device, so a backup file or iCloud sync is the way to move them.

I forgot my backup password.

We cannot recover it; backups are encrypted with your password only.

Reminders are not arriving.

Check iOS Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ DocuTimer, Focus modes, and Settings ▸ Reminders ▸ Delivery time in the app.
