Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Direct answers to common questions about renewal tracking, privacy, and device compatibility.

What is the 6-month passport rule and why do ordinary calendar apps fail?
More than 70 international destinations (including the Schengen area, Thailand, Singapore, and parts of the Caribbean) refuse entry or board denial if your passport has fewer than 6 months of validity remaining from your intended departure date. If your calendar app notifies you on expiration day, you have already been barred from international travel for 6 months! DocuTimer calculates your true Renewal Runway™, alerting you before you hit the travel cutoff and factoring in typical 8–11 week passport processing backlogs.
Is my sensitive personal identity data secure?
100% yes. DocuTimer is engineered with zero-knowledge, local-first architecture. All documents and camera scans are encrypted with hardware-backed AES-256 on your iOS device and protected behind biometric Face ID or Touch ID. If you choose to enable iCloud synchronization, your records replicate strictly into your personal private CloudKit database container. We operate zero cloud servers, hold zero encryption keys, and Apple verifies: Data Not Collected.
How does the Camera MRZ scanner work?
DocuTimer uses Apple's Vision framework to read the Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) chevron text at the bottom of passports and ID cards. Simply point your camera, and the parser automatically fills the document holder name, document number, issuing country, birth date, and exact expiration date in under two seconds.
Can I track documents for multiple family members or dependents?
Yes. DocuTimer Pro includes complete Household tracking. You can create distinct profiles for your partner, children, dependent parents, or even individual vehicles. The Home screen features instant owner filter chips so you can check one person's upcoming deadlines or view the entire household at a glance.
What happens if I accidentally delete a document?
DocuTimer features a 30-day Trash Recovery Center. Any removed document remains safely restorable for 30 days before permanent deletion. Furthermore, you can export password-protected, encrypted PBKDF2 archive backups at any time in Settings.
Are notifications delivered reliably without internet?
Yes! All reminders are scheduled directly via local iOS notifications on your device. They fire reliably at a civilized 9:00 a.m. local time, even if you are offline, on airplane mode, or traveling internationally with roaming disabled.
